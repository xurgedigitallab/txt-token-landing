Introduction

TextRP is a communication platform designed to revolutionize the way users engage in messaging and digital interactions by integrating blockchain technology. Built on the XRP Ledger (XRPL), TextRP offers users secure, fast, and scalable messaging services across multiple networks and platforms.

TextRP bridges traditional messaging and decentralized technology, providing users with unique opportunities to engage with NFTs, DeFi, and other blockchain-based innovations. The platform fosters a digital ecosystem that rewards users for meaningful engagement while providing an efficient and privacy-focused communication tool.

At the heart of this ecosystem is the $TXT token, which powers the reward system and enables users to unlock features, purchase services, and participate in the broader decentralized economy.

Problem Statement

Traditional messaging platforms do not provide users with tangible rewards for engagement and offer limited utility beyond basic communication. They are often centralized, leading to concerns around data privacy, lack of transparency, and high operational costs.

mCredits: Account-Based Interaction Units

TextRP operates with mCredits—internal units that track user interactions. Unlike $TXT, mCredits are non-currency interaction units that regulate activity by managing the consumption of messaging and engagement services.

How mCredits Work

Users purchase mCredits using $TXT, $XRP, or fiat currency. Every message sent or widget interaction consumes mCredits. Users receive bonus mCredits when purchased with $TXT tokens.

The Role of $TXT

The $TXT token is central to the TextRP ecosystem, acting as both a reward currency and a utility token. It drives engagement, encourages participation, and powers the platform's economic infrastructure.

03
Chat Reward Distribution

Real-time Rewards

All $TXT minted from engagement is paid out immediately:

TextRP Chat: Every 24 hours at 12:00 AM GMT (batch payout of accumulated rewards).

TextRP Live!: At the end of each live stream session (instant XRPL transfer to creator + viewers).

Engagement Value

Platform actions consume mCredits and instantly mint $TXT at the current live rate (updated every 60 seconds):

In-App Messages • Live Stream Emoji reactions • Bridged Messaging • Widget Interactions

Every mCredit spent on an action mints real $TXT directly from the depleting reward pool, creating immediate, ownable value from personal attention.

Rolling 24-Hour Distribution

The total reward pool releases the tiered percentage of its current balance continuously over a rolling 24-hour window. This creates a smooth, always-on flow of new $TXT while preserving the growth-based scarcity structure.

This model delivers continuous, real-time tokenization of engagement across Live and messaging—providing instant feedback in Live and reliable daily rewards in chat.
